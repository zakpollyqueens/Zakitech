How it works

  1. Create an invoice and add its costs.
  2. Check the calculated total before sharing.
  3. Send the unique link to your customer or boss.
  4. They review the invoice and continue to JumaPay.
  5. Verified payment updates the invoice to Paid.
The payment-link generator never expires. Individual invoices can be configured for 7–365 days and each invoice is payable once.
