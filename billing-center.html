How it works

  1. Create a new invoice for a customer.
  2. Review the itemized costs and total.
  3. Generate a unique checkout link.
  4. Copy and send it through WhatsApp or ZakiChat.
  5. JumaPay payment is confirmed server-side before the invoice changes to Paid.
The payment-link generator never expires. Individual invoices can be configured for 7–365 days and each invoice is payable once.
